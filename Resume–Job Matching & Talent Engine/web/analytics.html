Randomized QuickSort Match Ranking

Sorts candidate compatibility rankings with random pivot selection (`ThreadLocalRandom`).

Expected Time Complexity: O(N log N)
Worst-Case Time Complexity: O(N²)
Rationale: Deterministic pivot degrades on pre-sorted arrays. Random pivot selection makes consistently bad partitions extraordinarily unlikely (∼ 2^N / N!).
